This morning, we invite you to celebrate surprise and wild curiosity. Our dear guest, [Patricia Dallio](https://patriciadallio.bandcamp.com/album/transmissions-fongist-re-001), is a French experimental electronic musician who began her career in the ’80s and spent three decades as a member of the now-cult band Art Zoyd. You might find her improvising and creating music somewhere in the forests of Haute-Marne. The vegetal world is buzzing with life and sound, if only one knows how to shift one’s attention toward the ever-magical world of the non-human. Be prepared to reassess some of your assumptions about what a MailTape episode can be.

In a world increasingly shaped by prediction and repetition, surprise becomes essential. Letting our musical tastes evolve, and remain in a state of metamorphosis, is life itself moving through our bodies. Music keeps changing because life keeps changing.

# Guest's selection

## [Albert Marcœur](https://www.labelfreres.com/) - Une belle mort

Patricia Dallio: **"** Je connais le travail d’Albert depuis longtemps, et comme il dit dans ses texte à propos de cet album, « allez encore un p’tit dernier avant de plier bagages » J’ai trouvé ça tellement touchant. Et puis cette chanson nous révèle son immense humour. J’adore son travail.

I’ve known Albert’s work for a long time, and as he says in his lyrics about this album, “Come on, just one last little one before we pack up and leave.” I found that so touching. And then this song reveals his immense sense of humor. I love his work. **"** 

## [Bob Vylan](https://bobvylan.bandcamp.com/album/bob-vylan-presents-the-price-of-life) - Big Man

Patricia Dallio: **"** Déjà, son pseudo vaut le détour à lui tout seul. Cette énergie là ne se trouve pas à tous les coins de rue. C’est un musicien qui s’engage dans ses textes et qui « envoie le pâté » comme on dit.
 
His stage name alone is worth the detour. You don’t find that kind of energy on every street corner. He’s a musician who really throws himself into his lyrics and “gives it everything he’s got,” as they say. **"** 

## [Lutz Glandien](https://lutzglandien.bandcamp.com/album/lost-in-rooms) - Four Bedrooms

Patricia Dallio: **"** On m’a parlé de lui un jour pour me dire qu’il avait un air de famille avec mon travail. J’admire ce disque pour le savant mélange des textes et des compositions.
Ce monsieur a travaillé avec beaucoup de grand·es musicien·nes. Je le découvre tardivement, quelle chance  !
 
Someone told me about him one day, saying that his work had a family resemblance to mine. I admire this album for its skillful blend of lyrics and compositions.
This gentleman has worked with many great musicians. I’m discovering him late—but what a lucky find! **"** 

# Curator's selection

## [Patricia Dallio](https://patriciadallio.bandcamp.com/album/transmissions-fongist-re-001) - écho_des_hyphes

 ImaCrea: **"** This track comes from her latest album, released this summer, [*Transmissions fongistère 00.1*](https://patriciadallio.bandcamp.com/album/transmissions-fongist-re-001). Since the album is a complete listening experience, it wasn’t easy to choose just one track. I love this one for its intricate textures and evocative musical landscapes. I could say the same about any track on the album, really, but there’s something about this one, something I can’t fully explain, that makes me want to share it with you in particular. It instantly transports me to another space, allowing wild visions to emerge. **"** 

## [Lucie Antunes](lucieantunes.bandcamp.com/album/silence) - Mushrooms

 ImaCrea: **"** I can’t wait to see Lucie Antunes’ performance, created with choreographer Mathilde Monnier and featuring many other great artists: [*Silence*](https://www.festival-automne.com/fr/edition-2026/mathilde-monnier-lucie-antunes-silence). I’m literally counting down the days! The organic vibrancy and adrenaline rush of this track make it the perfect follow-up to the previous one. Also, don't forget to (re)check [her episode 384](http://localhost:4321/384/lucie-antunes/), from 2019 (!).**"** 

## [Patricia Dallio](https://patriciadallio.bandcamp.com/album/transmissions-fongist-re-001) - forêt_souterraine_fragments

 ImaCrea: **"** And now, after the mushrooms, let’s dive into the underground forest beneath our feet, where everything happens at once, synchronized by the fungal spirit. **"** 

## [Porta's](https://poorrecords.bandcamp.com/album/largent-du-n-pal) - Saccage dit

 ImaCrea: **"** Porta's is Sylvie Balestra + Nicolas Godin + Thomas Bernard. It was 2am yesterday as I was patiently curating music and assembling this episode. 4 hours in the making and plunged in almost a state of pseudo dream induced by Patricia's music, I started to listen to [*L'Argenté du Népal*](https://poorrecords.bandcamp.com/album/largent-du-n-pal) and again everything changed. Vivid light coming from this ecstatic track ended turning this musical journey into a new chapter filled with so many promises of profund discoveries. Death is not the end.**"**
